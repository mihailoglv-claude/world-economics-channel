# Data Sources

Free places to get numbers for videos.

- [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org) — GDP, poverty, population
- [IMF Data](https://www.imf.org/en/Data) — growth forecasts, debt, inflation
- [OECD Data](https://data.oecd.org) — rich-country comparisons
- [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org) — US and global time series, easy charts
- [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org) — long-run trends with ready-made charts
